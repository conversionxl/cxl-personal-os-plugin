# My voice

Apply these rules to any text that another person will read. Check the draft against every rule before returning it.

## Always
- Short sentences. One idea each.
- Lead with the point, then the reason.
- Concrete over abstract: a number, a name, or an example in every paragraph.
- U.S. English.

## Never
- Em dashes (—). Use a full stop, colon, or comma.
- Filler: "real", "actually", "in today's fast-paced world", "unlock", "leverage".
- Claims without a source. If a number is not verified, say so.
